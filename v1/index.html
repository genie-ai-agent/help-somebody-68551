Help Somebody is a small fund with one job: back the kind of person who’d go build the thing whether we wrote the check or not, and make the road a little shorter.

01

What we do

We write early checks to founders, builders, artists, and operators we believe in. Sometimes it’s a company. Sometimes it’s a project, a move, a leap. The unit is the person.

We don’t need a deck. We don’t need a data room. We need to understand who you are, what you’re trying to do, and what a little air cover would unlock.

02

Principles

  1. People over pitches.

    Character compounds. We’re looking for the person, not the plan.

  2. Move at the speed of trust.

    If it’s a yes, it’s a yes fast. If it’s a no, you’ll hear it.

  3. Small checks, real help.

    We’re not trying to lead your round. We’re trying to help you win.

  4. Show up when it’s hard.

    The check is the easy part. We stick around for the ugly middle.

  5. Pay it forward.

    The best returns we’ve ever gotten are from people who went on to help somebody else.

Get in touch

The best way in is a short note. Tell us who you are and what you’re working on. A paragraph beats a deck.

You can also post a Jelly and tag us. We watch. Sometimes the fastest way to get a yes is to just show us the thing.
