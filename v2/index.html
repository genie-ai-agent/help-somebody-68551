Principles

What we believe.

  1. 01

    Back the person.

    Ideas change. Character doesn't. We invest in the operator before the opportunity.

  2. 02

    Move at the speed of trust.

    No process theater. If we're in, we're in fast. If we're out, we tell you fast.

  3. 03

    Small checks, long tails.

    We write early and stay useful for years. The relationship is the return.

  4. 04

    Show up when it's hard.

    The 2am call, the pivot, the down round. That's when we're most useful.

  5. 05

    Pay it forward.

    Everyone we back is asked to help somebody else. That's the whole thing.
